## Full Description (4000 chars max)

OXIA is a gentle companion for your breathing practice. Whether you're looking to calm your mind, reduce stress, or simply become more aware of how you breathe — OXIA is here to support you.

**What OXIA offers:**

• 10+ guided breathing techniques — from box breathing and 4-7-8 to diaphragmatic breathing and alternate nostril. Each technique is explained clearly so you understand what it does and why it helps.

• Voice-guided sessions — follow along with calm, step-by-step audio guidance. Choose your voice and adjust the volume to suit your preference.

• Background music — soft ambient soundscapes to help you focus and settle in. Turn it on, off, or adjust the volume as you like.

• Track your progress — see how your practice is building over time. View your session history, streaks, and consistency at a glance.

• Emotional check-ins — optionally note how you feel before and after each session. Over time, OXIA helps you see patterns in your emotional wellbeing.

• Learning resources — short, accessible articles on why breathing matters, how it affects your nervous system, and practical tips for daily life.

• AI wellness reflections — receive thoughtful summaries of your practice patterns and emotional shifts over time.

• Customisable exercises — create your own breathing patterns with personalised inhale, hold, and exhale durations.

• Daily reminders — set gentle notifications to keep your practice consistent.

**Why breathing matters:**

Your breath is one of the few things that bridges your conscious and unconscious body. When you breathe with intention, you're directly influencing your nervous system — calming the fight-or-flight response and supporting your body's natural ability to rest and recover.

Regular breathing practice has been shown to help with:
- Reducing everyday stress and tension
- Improving sleep quality
- Supporting emotional balance
- Enhancing focus and clarity
- Lowering resting heart rate over time

**Built with care:**

OXIA was born from personal experience with anxiety and stress. It's designed to be a calm, pressure-free space — no upselling, no guilt, no streaks that punish you. Just a simple tool that helps you breathe better.

Your data stays private. Your practice is yours.

Breathe with awareness and ease.
